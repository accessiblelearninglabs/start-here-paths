Accessible Learning Labs · Start here path · The practitioner’s path

The chemistry sets the pace. Not the room.

The practitioner is the person whose judgement shapes how access happens. This path follows one, an accessibility advisor, and one request from the day it reaches the desk to the change it leaves in the institution. Seven stops; at each, the question, the resources that answer it, and what a good decision looks like. Most requests are decided at a desk like this one, one at a time, and how each is reasoned decides what the next learner meets. A workplace accommodation specialist or a patient accessibility coordinator meets the same questions at different milestones.

Determinations in individual accommodation cases rest with accessibility advisors, accommodation offices, and legal counsel; this path is offered in support of that work, never in place of it.
