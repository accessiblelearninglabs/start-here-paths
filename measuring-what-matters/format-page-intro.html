Accessible Learning Labs · Start here path · The practitioner’s path

The chemistry sets the pace. Not the room.

The practitioner is the person whose judgement shapes how access happens. This path follows one, an accessibility advisor, and one accommodation request from the day it reaches the advisor’s desk to the change it leaves behind in the institution. Seven stops; at each, the question the practitioner faces, the resources that answer it, and what a good decision looks like. A workplace accommodation specialist or a patient accessibility coordinator meets the same questions at different milestones. It is a way into the landscape of seventy-three knowledge resources for anyone who would rather begin with a case than with a map.

Determinations in individual accommodation cases rest with accessibility advisors, accommodation offices and legal counsel; this path is offered in support of that work, never in place of it.
