Three ways to read this

Read the path through.

It stands on its own. Nothing needs to be opened to follow the case from the first stop to the seventh.

Read the path, then stay at one stop.

Open the stop that matches where you are, with its resources beside it. The hard call is the stop most readers return to.

Use the seven stops as a reading order.

Taken in sequence, their resources touch every group on the landscape, from grounding and rights to standards and institutional change.
