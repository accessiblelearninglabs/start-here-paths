Accessible Learning Labs · Start here path · The expert’s path

The duty begins with the email. Not the letter.

The expert is the person who holds what someone else needs to reach: a subject, a skill, a service. Expertise in a subject is not expertise in a participant’s access; that belongs to the participant. This path follows one expert, an instructor, and one request from the evening it arrives, two weeks before any letter, to the change it leaves in the course and the institution. Seven stops; at each, the question, the resources that answer it, and what a good decision looks like. What the expert does before any office is involved decides what the request costs the person who made it. A doctor or a facilitator meets the same questions at different milestones.

Determinations in individual accommodation cases rest with accessibility advisors, accommodation offices, and legal counsel; this path is offered in support of that work, never in place of it. What an instructor can do on their own authority is set by their institution’s policy, and this path says where that line usually falls rather than where it falls at any one institution.
