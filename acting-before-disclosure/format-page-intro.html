Accessible Learning Labs · Start here path · The expert’s path

The duty begins with the email. Not the letter.

The expert is the person who holds what someone else needs to reach: a subject, a skill, a service. Expertise in a subject is not expertise in a participant’s access; that belongs to the participant. This path follows one expert, an instructor, and one accommodation request from the evening it arrives in the instructor’s inbox, two weeks before any letter, to the change it leaves in the course and the institution. Seven stops; at each, the question the instructor faces, the resources that answer it, and what a good decision looks like. A doctor or a facilitator meets the same questions at different milestones. It is a way into the landscape of seventy-three knowledge resources for anyone whose knowledge others rely on, and a companion to the practitioner’s path, which follows a different request from the other side of the desk.

Determinations in individual accommodation cases rest with accessibility advisors, accommodation offices and legal counsel; this path is offered in support of that work, never in place of it. What an instructor can do on their own authority is set by their institution’s policy, and this path says where that line usually falls rather than where it falls at any one institution.
