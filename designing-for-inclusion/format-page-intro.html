Accessible Learning Labs · Start here path · The leader’s path

The barrier came with the licence. The duty did not.

The leader is the person who answers for the system everyone else works inside. This path follows one, the associate dean responsible for a faculty’s teaching laboratories, and one request from the day it arrives, four months before the learners it concerns, to the change it leaves in how the institution buys, builds and assesses. Seven stops; at each, the question the leader faces, the resources that answer it, and what a good decision looks like. An employer or a hospital director meets the same questions at different milestones. It is a way into the landscape of seventy-three knowledge resources for anyone who answers for what an organization requires, buys and builds, and a companion to the practitioner’s path, which follows a request from the desk where it is usually decided.

Determinations in individual accommodation cases rest with accessibility advisors, accommodation offices and legal counsel; this path is offered in support of that work, never in place of it. It follows the decisions that belong to whoever holds an institution’s authority: what is required of every learner, what is funded, what is bought, and who answers for each.
