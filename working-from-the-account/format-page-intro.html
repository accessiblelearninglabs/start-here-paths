Accessible Learning Labs · Start here path · The participant’s path

The system wants a history. The account is evidence.

The participant is the person seeking to take part, and the one who knows their own access best. This path follows one, Avery, a learner, and one request from the week Avery writes it to the change it leaves behind. Seven stops; at each, the question, the resources that answer it, and what a good decision looks like. Whether a request is made at all is decided here, before any office is involved. A patient or an employee meets the same questions at different milestones.

This path describes what the law and professional guidance say an institution owes, and how a request is commonly handled. It is not legal advice, and it does not replace an advisor, an advocate, or legal counsel. Determinations in individual accommodation cases rest with accessibility advisors and accommodation offices.
